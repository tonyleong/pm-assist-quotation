1. What you are buying

Sorted so you can ship an MVP first. Internal AI Chatbot and SOP Knowledge Base must be taken together — published SOP text is guarded; staff only get it by asking the chatbot, which does not guess.

MVP workflow: publish SOP → staff ask → cited answer. The AI only uses approved information. If it cannot find an answer, it tells staff to confirm with management. Documents & OCR is not required for MVP — admin can type or paste SOP text.
